Board of Pharmacy Proposed Rulemaking Chapter 17 and Chapter 29
The Maine Board of Pharmacy is proposing changes to two chapters of its rules:
Chapter 17, Licensure and Operation of Nuclear Drug Outlets (Amend)
Chapter 29, Violations of State or Federal Law or Rule; Other Standards (Repeal and Replace)
The proposed rulemaking will update the state and federal statutes, regulations, rules and certain chapters of the United States
Pharmacopeia that the board adopts as established standards of professional behavior in the practice of pharmacy and the operation of pharmacies.
The proposed rulemaking will incorporate by reference certain standards in the United States Pharmacopeia and National Formulary (USP-NF),
including but not limited to General Chapter, 825 Radiopharmaceuticals – Preparation, Compounding and Repacking in Chapter 17, which
applies to the operation of Nuclear Drug Outlets.
