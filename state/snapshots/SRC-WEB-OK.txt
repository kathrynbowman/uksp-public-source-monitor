Published rules for the Oklahoma State Board of Pharmacy
Secretary of State Rules Code homepage
The permanent rules of the Oklahoma State Board of Pharmacy have been codified in Title 535 of the Oklahoma Administrative Code (OAC). The OAC is published by the Office of Administrative Rules, a department of the Secretary of State, and contains all "official" agency rules. "Official" copies of the laws regarding pharmacy may be obtained from the statute books.
