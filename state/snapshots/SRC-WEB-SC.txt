Board-Approved Final Proposed Regulations for Compounding and Collaborative Practice Agreements
The South Carolina Board of Pharmacy approved proposed regulations for compounding practices and for
collaborative practice agreements at the called meeting on October 8, 2026. These regulations will
be published in the State Register in October. The Board intends to submit these to the General
Assembly at the start of the next legislative session.
The notice in the State Register will contain a Opportunity for Public Comment. Written comments may
be directed to Kayce Shealy, Chief Drug Inspector and Board Executive, Board of Pharmacy, South
Carolina Department of Labor, Licensing and Regulation, Post Office Box 11329, Columbia, South
Carolina 29211-1289, no later than 5:00 p.m., November 23, 2026.
