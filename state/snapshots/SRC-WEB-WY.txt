Proposed Wyoming Controlled Substances Act Rules Revisions
The Wyoming State Board of Pharmacy proposes to amend Chapter 8: Prescription Drug Monitoring Program, of the Wyoming Controlled Substances Act Rules and Regulations to simplify data retention in the controlled substance prescription tracking program.
As required by Wyoming Statute § 16-3-103(a)(i)(G), these rules meet minimum substantive state statutory requirements.
Chapter 8: Prescription Drug Monitoring Program
Section 8(c) is revised so that records shall be maintained in the database for five (5) years. Currently, records that are older than five (5) years, are deidentified and archived for research purposes. Deidentification removes information so that the dispensing data cannot be associated with an individual patient. This process removes most of the data’s research value and cannot be undone. Storing data indefinitely will increase the database’s costs. The proposed revision will help to keep the database’s costs low and aligns with the Chapter’s other data retention requirements, e.g. records of solicited and unsolicited patient reports which are kept for five (5) years.
The proposed revisions also include minor grammatical/non-substantive changes:
Bringing the chapter name below the chapter number to match the formatting requested by the Secretary of State’s office;
Adding a missing period to the sentence in section 2; and
Adding an apostrophe in Section 5(a)(ix)
