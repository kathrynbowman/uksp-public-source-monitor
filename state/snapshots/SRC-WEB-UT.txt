Administrative Rule Updates and Executive Notices
The
Utah State Bulletin
(Bulletin) is an official publication of the Office of Administrative Rules, mandated by
Section 63G-3-402
. It is Utah's equivalent to the Federal Register. The Bulletin includes proposed rules, rule analyses, notices of effective dates, and review notices, as well as Governor’s executive documents. The Bulletin contains the full text of administrative rules and is an electronic publication issued on the 1st and 15th of each month. A summary of the Bulletin, the
Utah State Digest
, is also available online and by email.
For information on authenticating files from the Office, as well as ways to generate MD5 hash codes for comparison, refer to
Researching Administrative Rules - Authentication
.
